# The design that answers him

Stance: a cabin cinema in the Colorado woods at night. Dark like a theater, lit warm.

- Ground: deep pine night, not black. Warm bark and parchment surfaces. One sunset ember for "plays".
- Texture: a topographic contour map behind sections (Colorado, Middle-earth maps). Paper grain on parchment.
- Type: a carved park-sign slab for titles (rustic, LOTR inscription feel, centered like a Grand Budapest title card), Futura-like Jost for small labels (Wes Anderson).
- Grand Budapest: symmetry, framed title cards, a ticket for each featured film.
- Whimsy (Mad Max, Squatch "silly"): the ticket tilts and lifts when picked, a sun badge that turns while the song plays, a hand-lettered aside or two. Never salesy copy.
- The song: "Sowing the Seeds of Love". Sun and seeds as the motif: the reel opens on a sun flare, the sound toggle is a little sun.
- Nature: his own landscape photos carry About; the photo changes with each chapter.
